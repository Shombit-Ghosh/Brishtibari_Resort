Welcome to Brishtibari Resort, your private home away from home. Set right in the middle of a lush, verdant tea garden, our property gives you front-row seats to nature's finest green canopy framed by the historic Baikunthapur Forest.

Whether you want to sip hot local tea while listening to the raindrops filter through the leaves, take a peaceful dip in our pool, or explore the sacred spiritual landmarks nearby, Brishtibari offers a matchless rustic yet luxurious escape.
