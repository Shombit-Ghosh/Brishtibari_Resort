Untamed Territory: Wild Elephant Crossings

Brishtibari is located in an active migratory and foraging corridor within the Baikunthapur Forest. It is common for majestic wild elephants to visit the property boundaries under the cover of dusk. Nightlife here is intensely bone-chilling, immersive, and purely raw—accompanied by rustling forest canopies, distant calls, and absolute isolation from the city lights.

Where Mist, Tea Gardens, and Darkness Meet

Welcome to Brishtibari Resort, isolated in the deep core of Bodagonj, Jalpaiguri. By day, enjoy the tranquil breeze rolling over miles of endless green tea leaves. By night, the atmosphere transforms into a deeply mysterious, eerie, and atmospheric wilderness.

With zero urban light pollution, the night sky is breathtakingly clear, yet heavy with the spine-tingling thrill of living right alongside the deep jungle. Listen to the symphony of nocturnal predators, insects, and occasional heavy footsteps outside your window.

The Crown Jewel: Jungle Oasis Pool

The undisputed heart of Brishtibari Resort is our stunning swimming pool. Situated directly between the shadows of the dense Baikunthapur jungle and the edge of the tea garden, it offers a surreal swimming experience.

Float under the moonlight with the dense forest surrounding you on all sides. Whether you're taking a cool morning dip wrapped in thick jungle mist or stargazing during a bone-chilling night swim, this pool delivers an unforgettable adventure.
