Raw Forest Living & Wild Elephant Visits

Brishtibari is completely enveloped by the dense, untouched cover of the Baikunthapur forest. Living here means absolute closeness to rich green nature. Under the cover of twilight and midnight silence, majestic wild elephants frequently cross through the perimeter, giving you a thrilling, bone-chillingly authentic encounter with the wild.

Living Inside a Living Green Canopy

At Brishtibari Resort, greenery isn't just a view—it's an atmosphere you live inside. Every window opens up to rolling waves of dark green tea gardens merging directly into towering ancient trees and emerald ferns.

When rain falls through the thick overhead canopy, the forest comes alive with the earthy scent of wet moss and rustling leaves. By night, the dense foliage creates an eerily quiet, mysterious ambiance, far removed from the noise of civilization.

The Crown Jewel: Emerald Oasis Pool

The crown jewel of our property is the striking swimming pool, carved right into the green heart of the resort. Surrounded on all sides by dense forest trees and tea bushes, it is the ultimate spot to relax.

Take a refreshing swim surrounded by vibrating walls of green, or experience a bone-chilling night swim under the starlit sky while the jungle whispers secrets in the dark shadows around you.
